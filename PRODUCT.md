# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors include recruiters, collaborators, classmates, and people arriving from GitHub who want to understand Shuqi Ke as both a technical builder and a person. They are scanning for credible capability in computer vision, edge AI, and robotics, while also looking for signals of curiosity, taste, and lived interests.

## Product Purpose

This homepage presents Shuqi Ke's world: technical work, project outcomes, GitHub activity, and personal life interests in one coherent portfolio. Success means visitors can quickly read the page, understand the technical focus, feel the personal richness, and find projects, resume, GitHub, or contact paths without friction.

## Brand Personality

Professional and reliable, interesting, and rich with inner context. The page should feel capable and thoughtful without becoming either visually noisy or overly plain.

## Anti-references

Avoid pages that are too flashy, overly decorative, hard to read, or visually crowded. Also avoid pages that feel too plain, generic, resume-template-like, or empty of personality.

## Design Principles

- Clarity before spectacle: text must be easy to read, and the layout must make scanning effortless.
- Technical credibility through evidence: projects, repositories, and resume paths should be concrete and easy to reach.
- Personal world, not a side note: life, games, music, and reading should feel intentionally integrated with technical identity.
- Rich but controlled: visual layers can create depth, but spacing, hierarchy, and contrast must keep the page calm.
- Every section needs a job: each fold should clarify who Shuqi is, what he builds, or how to follow up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA as a baseline, with special attention to readable contrast, keyboard navigation, touch target size, reduced-motion behavior, and layouts that remain clear on mobile and desktop.
